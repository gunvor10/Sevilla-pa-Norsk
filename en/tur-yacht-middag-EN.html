In short

A cruise on the Guadalquivir aboard a yacht, with a waiter and a skipper on board. You choose two or three hours, for lunch or dinner. On board you are served a selection of Sevillian tapas — pata negra ham, salmorejo, Spanish potato omelette and more — along with soft drinks, beer, manzanilla from the region, coffee, tea and water. There is 30 minutes of live Spanish music on the way, rumbas and sevillanas. You see the city from the river: Torre del Oro, the Triana bridge, the San Telmo palace and Torre Sevilla. There are two ways to do it: join other guests, 14 on board at most, or charter the whole yacht exclusively for your own party — no other guests, your own menu, your own time. Departure from the Muelle de la O pier. From €120 per person when sharing; the price for the whole boat is quoted on request. 2026 prices. Booking goes through me — send an enquiry and I will set it up.

my favourite on the river

About the cruise

Seville grew rich on the river. All the gold from the Americas came in here, and the city grew up around the harbour. Seeing it from the water is not only a good view; it is the direction the city was built to be seen from.

Here you need do nothing but come aboard. The waiter and the skipper take care of the rest. The food is put together as a gourmet menu of Spanish dishes, the wine is poured while the boat glides down the river, and the music is played live, not through a speaker.

If you want the boat entirely to yourselves, it is available for exclusive charter: no other guests on board, your own menu, your own time, and the deck decorated as you wish. This is what people choose for a birthday, an anniversary, a proposal or a honeymoon. If you are a smaller party, you can join other guests instead — same boat, same menu, shared with a few others.
