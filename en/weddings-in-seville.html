Arranging weddings for Norwegian couples in Spain is one of the most rewarding things I do. I know the most beautiful places, the suppliers, and all the little secret details that make the big day unforgettable. Norwegian warmth — Spanish magic.

From the first idea to the last dance

Through Sevilla Event Luxury Weddings, I take care of everything — from the very first idea until you dance the last dance. After more than 30 years in Andalucía, I know the most beautiful palaces, haciendas, florists and the skilled suppliers needed to make it all come together.

And most importantly: you can speak Norwegian with me the whole way. I translate, coordinate, and take care of the thousand little details, so you can be fully present on your own day.
