In short

A day trip of around nine hours with a private car and driver. We pick you up at your hotel in Seville about 09:30 and drive north-west, up into the Sierra of Aracena. First a family-run farm where you see how the pigs are reared and how much the oak trees matter. Then a guided visit to one of the best ham producers in the town of Jabugo, where an expert explains the whole process — and thousands upon thousands of hams hang slowly curing. About the tour

Iberian, or “ibérico”, ham has become more and more well-known over the last few years, which is no surprise as it is the best ham in the world, especially if it is the acorn-fed variety. On this trip we take you to one of the most important areas for the production of Iberian ham in Spain, the Sierra of Aracena, a mountainous area north-west of Seville near to the Portuguese border. These hams are cured in the mountain air for up to three years and are considered by many people to be the jewel in the crown of Spanish gastronomy.

We first visit a family-run farm in a beautiful setting to see how the pigs are reared and the importance that oak trees have. We then go on to see the installations of one of the best ham producers in the town of Jabugo, a mythical name in Spain for fine ham. An expert shows us around, explaining the processes involved in the production of ham and of the other products which come from the Iberian pig. You will be completely taken aback when you see the thousands upon thousands of hams hanging up and slowly curing. At the end of the visit you taste the wonderful ham along with a glass of the best fino or manzanilla, a perfect pairing of food and wine.

Afterwards we go on to the town of Aracena for a tapas lunch at a local restaurant. We have known the place since 1995 and the owners are great friends of ours. All we do when we arrive is to sit down and wait for the food to come, not knowing what they will serve us. Dishes might include asparagus, artichokes, wild mushrooms, cheese, acorn-fed Iberian pork and many other possibilities that change with the season, as everything is local. The food is accompanied by fine Spanish wine and ends with some great dessert. After all the food a walk up to the castle on the hill is in order before we head back to Seville.
