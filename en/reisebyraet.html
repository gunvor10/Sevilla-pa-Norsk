A little about my agency

I started Flavors of Andalucia in 2020 to open the doors of Spain to American travellers and to anyone who cares about the unique and exciting culture of the south — not just the famous destinations, but the real, local experience you only get with someone who lives here. After more than 30 years in Seville, I know the country, the suppliers, and every small detail that makes a trip unforgettable.

Whether you're a group of good friends, a company on a trip, or a couple wanting to experience Andalucía on your own — I tailor everything from scratch and am there for you the whole way.
