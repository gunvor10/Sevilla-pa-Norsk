Who am I?

Hi, I'm Gunvor — Norwegian in Seville

Resident since 1994 · Licensed guide · Travel agency owner

  • —Only official Norwegian guide in Seville
  • —Four travel brands: Spaniaopplevelser, DMC Seville Event, Flavors of Andalucia and Sevilla Event Luxury Weddings
  • —Arranges weddings in historic palaces
  • —Leading Camino de Santiago groups since 2015
  • —Airbnb host with two rooms in Seville

A random road trip that changed everything

It actually started as a spontaneous idea. I was working for Ving in Albufeira, Portugal, and my colleagues wouldn't stop: "You HAVE to visit Seville!" At the first opportunity, I rented a car and drove there. Little did I know that trip would change my life forever — for reasons I hadn't quite foreseen.

I stayed in Seville. And I haven't regretted it for a second.

I had always worked in the travel industry, and Seville is a city that appeals to more than just tourists. I remember many times walking around and thinking: "Oh, how beautiful this is." It wasn't hard to settle in a city that offers beauty around every single street corner.

Who is Gunvor?

I'm from Bergen — so I'm used to rain, and appreciate Seville's sun all the more for it. I have two grown children who have both grown up between Norwegian and Spanish culture, something I'm very proud of.

In my free time I dance swing, care about healthy food — luckily Seville has fantastic access to fresh produce — and I'm fascinated by history, which fits perfectly in a city with 3,000 years behind it. Perhaps it's exactly that interest in history that inspired me to start guiding in Seville.

I also like hiking in beautiful nature — although I won't find Bergen's magnificent mountains here, you can travel about an hour from Seville to find beautiful chestnut forests and oak plains where Iberian pigs roam free. It's a side of Andalucía many don't know about, but well worth discovering.

From studies to my own agency

I studied tourism in Seville and began working for a major Spanish tour operator, especially with conferences, corporate trips and incentive travel — the kind of trips I still enjoy working with the most today. There's something special about creating experiences that people remember for the rest of their lives.

In 2010 I started my own travel agency and opened the doors of all of Spain to Norwegians. The Basque Country with San Sebastián and Bilbao, wine tours to La Rioja and Ribera del Duero, the Camino de Santiago for active women — and experiences in Málaga, Barcelona, Madrid and Alicante. On the first Camino de Santiago trip, I safely guided my uncle and his group of friends all the way to Santiago. Since then there have been many more.

Back to Seville — and out into the world

I longed to work more in Seville and show off this wonderful city — not just to Norwegians, but to travellers from all over the world. New products saw the light of day, and I started DMC Seville Event. With great pleasure I now welcome incentive groups from Northern Europe and the USA and show them Seville's maravillas — the city's wonders.

To be able to show Norwegians Seville in Norwegian, I picked up the history books and became the only official Norwegian guide in Seville. It's a title I'm very proud of.

Over time, many requests came in for weddings in Seville — for this is a romantic city that inspires love. I started Sevilla Event Luxury Weddings and today arrange beautiful weddings in historic palaces in Seville or charming haciendas in the surrounding area.

Seville in a Nutshell

My Favourite Spots

Places I love — that you won't find in every guidebook. After 30 years, I have my own personal favourites.

Market
Mercado de Triana
The liveliest market in the city — fresh fish, olives and a coffee among locals. Best early in the morning.
Neighbourhood
Barrio Santa Cruz
The old Jewish quarter — a maze of alleys where I still get lost, on purpose.
Architecture
Plaza de España
One of Spain's most beautiful squares. Come at sunset, when the tiles glow.
Food & drink
My secret tapas bar
The little bar with no sign, where they still remember my name. You get the address if you come with me.
Nature
Río Guadalquivir
A walk along the river at dusk — my favourite way to end the day.
Gardens
The Alcázar gardens
The palace gardens with orange trees, fountains and peacocks. Pure magic early in the day.
