Frequently asked questions

Are you an officially licensed guide in Seville?

Yes, I am the only officially licensed Norwegian guide in Seville and Andalucía.

Which languages are the tours held in?

I guide in English and Norwegian myself. German, Italian, French, Polish and Portuguese are available through licensed colleagues I work with regularly.

How long does a typical tour take?

Tours usually last 2–4 hours, but can be extended to a full day, as in the "A day with me in Seville" programme.

Can tours be tailored for groups and families?

Yes, all tours can be adapted to group size, pace and interests — from couples to larger corporate groups.
