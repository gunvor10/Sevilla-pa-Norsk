A little about me

I’m Gunvor

I’m 58, from Bergen in Norway, and I have lived in Seville for over 30 years. I run the travel agency Flavors of Andalucia, I’m an official guide in Seville and Andalucía, I plan weddings in Spain, and I rent out two rooms in my home.

I love this city — the white houses, the orange trees, the sound of flamenco from an open door on an ordinary Tuesday. It’s the local, slightly secret version I want to share with you.

Questions and answers

What people often ask me

Do you guide the tours yourself?+

Yes. I guide the city tours in Seville myself, in English and Norwegian. If I’m not available, a licensed colleague takes the tour — they also guide in German, Italian, French, Polish and Portuguese.

Can I join a tour on my own?+

My tours are for private parties and groups. If you are travelling alone, you’ll find shared tours and day trips I recommend on the tours page.

Do you plan weddings?+

Yes, in Seville, and in palaces and haciendas in Alicante, Málaga and Marbella.

Can we stay with you?+

I rent out two rooms in my home in Seville. You’ll find them on the rooms page.

How do I book?+

Send an email to gunvor@spaniaopplevelser.com or a WhatsApp message to +34 637 87 87 68, and I’ll get back to you.
