A little about me

I love this city — the whitewashed houses, the orange trees, the sound of flamenco drifting from an open door on an ordinary Tuesday. After 30 years, I know Seville like the back of my hand, and it's that local, slightly secret version of it that I want to share with you.

On this blog I share everyday life as it really is — my favourite tapas bars, my favourite places, and everything I wish someone had told me when I first arrived.

– Gunvor 🍊
