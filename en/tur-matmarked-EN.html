In short

A private food tour in Seville of about four hours with seven stops, guided in English or Norwegian by Gunvor Guttormsen, an officially licensed guide in Andalucía. We meet in front of the town hall on Plaza Nueva and start with a typical Andalusian breakfast — toast with ham and tomato, freshly squeezed orange juice and coffee — then churros, Triana market, an olive tasting, Iberian ham with cheese and wine, an olive oil shop with tasting, and tapas at one of the city's oldest tapas bars. Guide 195 € for the whole party, food and drink 65 € per person. The tour runs Monday to Saturday and is adapted to allergies and food preferences.

About the tour

Seville makes most sense through its food. Not in the restaurants with photographs on the menu, but where the city itself eats: over breakfast at the counter in the morning, between the stalls at the market, and in a tapas bar that has been there longer than anyone remembers.

We begin with breakfast the way it looks here: toast with ham and tomato, orange juice squeezed while you wait, and coffee. Then churros. After that we cross the river to Triana and walk through the market, before tasting our way through olives, hand-carved Iberian ham, cheese from the mountains and a glass of wine. We stop at an olive oil shop for a tasting, and finish with tapas. Along the way I explain why the city eats the way it does: what the Moors left behind, what came home on the ships from the Americas, and why tapas were invented in the first place.

Every stop is a place I go to myself. We take the time we need — this is not a tour that rushes on, and you will not leave any of it hungry.

How the day goes

Breakfast like a local

Churros

Stop 3
Triana market
We cross the river and wander through the market in Triana, where the stalls stand shoulder to shoulder.
Stop 4
Olives
An olive tasting — several varieties, and you notice quickly that they are nothing like the ones at home.

Ham, cheese and wine

Olive oil

Tapas to finish
