Seville's best food markets

By Gunvor · August 16, 2026 · 7 min read
where locals shop

If you want to understand a city, go to the market. Seville's food markets are where the locals actually shop — not just a place for tourists to take photos. Here are my favourites.

Mercado de Triana

Built on the ruins of the old Castillo de San Jorge, this is my favourite market. Here you'll find a cooking school, a small theatre, and stalls with everything from fresh fish to Iberico ham. A perfect combination of history, culture and food, right in the heart of Triana.

Mercado de la Feria

Seville's oldest market, dating back to the 13th century. Located in the lively Macarena neighbourhood, far from the tourist crowds, and therefore one of the most authentic places to see how Sevillians actually shop for food.

Mercado del Arenal

Right by the Maestranza bullring, with a long history as a fish market. Today it's a good mix of traditional stalls and places to sit down for a quick tapa or a glass of wine mid-shop.

Mercado de la Encarnación (Setas)

Beneath the famous "Setas de Sevilla" — the great wooden structure in the centre — sits a modernised market with both traditional stalls and more spacious eateries. Its convenient location makes it a natural stop mid-sightseeing.

«A market tells you more about a city than any monument. Here you see what people actually eat, not just what they show off.»

Lonja del Barranco

A gourmet market right on the riverbank, housed in an old 19th-century iron structure designed by Gustave Eiffel's team. The atmosphere here is more modern and social than the traditional markets — perfect for a drink and a bite as the sun sets over the Guadalquivir.

Mercado Puerta de la Carne

One of the city's oldest marketplaces, renovated and reopened in a beautiful historic building near Santa Cruz. Combines traditional stalls with a good selection of eateries, and makes a nice stop after a walk through the old town.

Mercado de Los Remedios

A neighbourhood market in the well-off Los Remedios area, less visited by tourists than the others. Here Sevillians buy what they need for dinner — meat, fish and fresh vegetables, with no show, just good quality.

Tips for your market visit

Go early in the morning to see the market at its most authentic, before the tourists arrive. Most stalls only take cash for smaller purchases. And don't be shy — pointing, tasting and asking is a natural part of market culture in Seville.

«The markets are where Seville is still Seville — unfiltered, without staging. Just people buying food for dinner, as they have for generations.»

Frequently asked questions

Which market is most authentic in Seville?

Mercado de la Feria, Seville's oldest market, sits outside the usual tourist routes and is therefore one of the most authentic.

Can you pay by card at Seville's markets?

Most stalls prefer cash, especially for smaller purchases — it's good to bring some with you.

When is the best time to visit the food markets?

Early morning, before the tourist crowds and while the selection is still freshest.
