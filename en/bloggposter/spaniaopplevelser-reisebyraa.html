Spaniaopplevelser — how the adventure began

By Gunvor · May 20, 2025 · 7 min read
from redundancy to my own agency

Spaniaopplevelser is my travel agency in Seville, with the sister brands DMC Seville Event, Flavors of Andalucia and Sevilla Event Luxury Weddings. No one dreams of being laid off. But sometimes it's exactly in the moments you didn't choose yourself that the best stories begin.

2008 — the crisis that became the starting gun

It wasn't the plan. I'd worked in the travel industry my whole life. Since 2001 I'd been part of one of Spain's largest tourism groups, which had an office here in Seville. Then came 2008. The financial crisis hit Spain with a force few had foreseen, and the company had to cut back. I was offered voluntary redundancy. I had two small children at home — both very young.

«Sitting at home without a job simply wasn't an option. I knew what I could do — and I decided to use it.»

From home office to travel agency

And so it began — slowly, carefully, but with purpose. From a small home office in Seville, with two infants around her, Gunvor started building what would become Spaniaopplevelser. No big launch, no investors, no roadmap. Just knowledge, experience, a network — and an unshakeable belief that Spain deserves to be discovered properly.

Spaniaopplevelser — for Norwegians who want more

Today, Spaniaopplevelser helps Norwegians, Swedes and Danes discover Spain properly — not through a standardised charter package, but through tailor-made trips with real substance. From the Basque gastronomic capital San Sebastián in the north to authentic Andalucía in the south.

What sets us apart isn't just that we know Spain well. It's that we live here. That kind of knowledge, you can't Google your way to.

«We don't sell trips. We open doors to a Spain most people never get to see from a hotel window.»

More than one agency — four brands, one passion

As the business grew, Gunvor began building dedicated brands for different markets and needs. Today there are four:

What I'm most proud of

Looking back on the fifteen-odd years that have passed — it isn't the growth or the brands I'm proudest of. It's the people. The clients who called back and said that trip to Spain was the best they'd ever had. Couples who said their wedding day exceeded everything they'd dreamed of. That's what I wanted to build — and that's what we've done. Crisis and all.

Frequently asked questions

Which travel agency brands does Gunvor run?

Four brands: Spaniaopplevelser for Scandinavian travellers, DMC Seville Event for corporate travel, Flavors of Andalucia for private luxury tours in English, and Sevilla Event Luxury Weddings for destination weddings.

Who is Flavors of Andalucia for?

Travellers who want Seville and Andalucía privately, at their own pace, with access to places that are not open to everyone. In English.

Where are destination weddings held through Gunvor's agency?

In Seville for international couples, and in Alicante and Málaga for Norwegian couples.
