Best river activities on the Guadalquivir

By Gunvor · August 16, 2026 · 6 min read
the river that made the city

The Guadalquivir is the reason Seville became the city it is — once Spain's most important port, today the city's calm pulse. Here are my favourites for experiencing the river, whether you want to row, sail past it, or simply walk along the bank.

Contents
  1. Sightseeing boat
  2. Kayak and SUP
  3. Rowing clubs along the river
  4. Running and cycling along the bank

Sightseeing boat

The easiest way to see Seville from the river. Boats leave from Torre del Oro and take you past Triana, under the bridges, giving a completely different perspective on the city than you get on foot. A calm hour, perfect on a warm afternoon when you want to slow down.

Kayak and SUP

For those who want to be more active: kayak and SUP rental is available in several spots along the bank. Paddling past Torre del Oro and Triana in the morning light, before the city has fully woken up, is one of the things I think most visitors miss out on.

«Paddling past Torre del Oro in the morning light is something few tourists ever experience.»

Rowing clubs along the river

Seville has a long tradition of rowing, and several clubs along the river offer trial sessions for visitors. The Guadalquivir is ideal for rowing — wide, calm and sheltered — and you'll see local rowers training here early morning and late afternoon, all year round.

Running and cycling along the bank

The promenade along the river, on both sides, is where Sevillians themselves train. Flat, shady in parts, and with views of some of the city's finest buildings. One of the best ways to get to know the city's rhythm away from the tourist routes.

Sunset by Torre del Oro

And finally — no list of river activities without mentioning the simplest of all: sitting by Torre del Oro as the sun sets and the Triana bridge lights up. No ticket, no planning. Just the river, the light and the city.

«The Guadalquivir isn't just a backdrop for Seville — it's the reason the city exists. Spending time by or on the river is to understand the city a little better.»

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim in the Guadalquivir in Seville?

No, the river isn't set up for swimming within the city — but kayaking, SUP and rowing are popular and safe activities.

Where can you rent a kayak in Seville?

Several rental spots along the riverbank, both near Torre del Oro and in Triana, offer kayak and SUP rental by the hour or full day.

How long does a sightseeing tour on the river last?

Usually around an hour, with departures from Torre del Oro throughout the day.
