10 best tapas bars in Seville

By Gunvor · August 16, 2026 · 8 min read
my regular stops

Seville is the home of tapas, and after over 30 years here I have my established favourites. Here are ten bars I always come back to — with tips on what to order at each one.

El Rinconcillo

photo: El Rinconcillo

Seville's oldest bar — open since 1670. Order espinacas con garbanzos (spinach with chickpeas) and a cold beer. The history alone is worth the visit.

Eslava

photo: Eslava

A bit more modern, but their honey-glazed ribs are legendary. Worth the wait for a table.

Bodega Santa Cruz (Las Columnas)

photo: Bodega Santa Cruz

Right by the Cathedral, and somehow still authentic. Try the montadito de pringá.

Casa Morales

photo: Casa Morales

Old-school charm, cured meats hanging overhead, and great montaditos — eaten among wine barrels. A trip back in time.

Ovejas Negras

photo: Ovejas Negras

Trendy and creative. Their truffled aubergine and mini burgers are a must.

Dos de Mayo

photo: Dos de Mayo

Beloved by locals for big portions and great prices. Don't miss the grilled octopus.

«A good tapas bar isn't judged by the menu — it's felt in the atmosphere the moment you walk through the door.»

La Brunilda

photo: La Brunilda

Upscale tapas in a relaxed setting. Try the prawn tempura or Iberian pork cheek.

Bar Alfalfa

photo: Bar Alfalfa

Small, lively and full of charm. Perfect for a glass of wine and some jamón.

Las Golondrinas

photo: Las Golondrinas

Hidden in Triana. Their presa Ibérica is famous well beyond the neighbourhood.

Cervecería Giralda

photo: Cervecería Giralda

Located in an old Arab bath next to the Cathedral. The atmosphere and history make this place unique, whatever you order.

«The best way to experience Seville isn't to sit at one table all evening, but to move from bar to bar — a tapa here, a glass there. That's how we do it.»

Frequently asked questions

What is the oldest bar in Seville?

El Rinconcillo, open since 1670, is considered the city's oldest bar.

Where can you find authentic tapas bars near the Cathedral?

Bodega Santa Cruz (Las Columnas) and Cervecería Giralda are both right by the Cathedral and still keep an authentic atmosphere.

Which tapas bar should you visit in Triana?

Las Golondrinas, known for its presa Ibérica, is hidden in Triana.
