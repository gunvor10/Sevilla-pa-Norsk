The most beautiful sunset spots in Seville

By Gunvor · August 16, 2026 · 6 min read
my favourite moments

After more than 30 years in Seville, I've watched thousands of sunsets. Some spots always give something extra. Here are my favourites.

Setas de Sevilla

The city's great wooden structure has a viewing platform on top, and from here you watch the sun set over the entire old town — the Cathedral, the Giralda and Seville's rooftops in a single view. Pay a small ticket, and you get the best view in the city.

Torre del Oro and the riverbank

No ticket, no planning. Just sit by the river at the old gold tower, and watch the light shift over the Guadalquivir while the Triana bridge lights up on the other side.

«I've guided hundreds of sunsets, and every single one has felt a little different.»

Plaza de España

The façade glows like gold in the last daylight. Come right before sunset and watch the whole square slowly shift colour — from warm ochre to deep orange to soft pink.

Puente de Triana

Right in the middle of the bridge between the centre and Triana, you get views both ways — towards Torre del Oro on one side, towards Triana's colourful façades on the other. One of my favourite spots to end a day of guiding.

Boat trip on the Guadalquivir

and if you want to take it a little further…

From the water, Seville looks completely different. An evening boat trip on the Guadalquivir takes you past Torre del Oro, under the bridges and along Triana, as the sky shifts colour over the rooftops. One of the most relaxing ways to end the day.

Querencia rooftop terrace

One of my favourite rooftop terraces in the centre. A good cocktail in hand, views over the old town's rooftops, and an atmosphere that's perfect for rounding off a warm day right as the sun starts to set.

Rooftop dinner with a view

One of the finest ways to end a day of guiding: a long dinner on a rooftop terrace, with the Giralda lit up in the distance as the sky shifts colour. Perfect for groups celebrating something a little extra.

Torre Sevilla rooftop

Seville's tallest skyscraper has a rooftop bar at the top, and from there you see the whole city stretching out towards the horizon. A little off the usual tourist trail, but worth the trip for a completely different perspective on Seville at dusk.

«The sunset is the one part of the day Seville always gives you for free, no matter where you're standing in the city.»

Frequently asked questions

Where's the best place to watch the sunset in Seville?

Setas de Sevilla gives the most complete view over the old town, while Torre del Oro and Puente de Triana are free options by the river.

Does it cost anything to watch the sunset from Setas de Sevilla?

Yes, there's a small ticket to go up to the viewing platform.

Are there free options for watching the sunset?

Yes, Torre del Oro, Puente de Triana and Plaza de España are all free and easily accessible.
