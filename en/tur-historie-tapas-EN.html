About the tour

Seville is a city you can walk straight through without understanding what you are looking at. That is why I run this tour. We pass the famous monuments, but it is the stories that make the difference: who built what and why, what stood here before, and how three cultures have laid themselves down layer upon layer in the same street. I have lived here for over 30 years, and I tell it the way I came to know it myself, not read off a script.

I build the route around you. Tell me what you would like to see and I will put the tour together around it, at whatever pace suits you. If it is your first time in Seville, I show you how the city fits together, so you know where things are for the rest of your stay. If you have been here before, I take you where most people do not go. We are a small group, so there is plenty of time to ask what you are wondering about and to stop when something catches your interest.

And then there is the tasting, which to me is half the tour. We walk into a place where the locals eat themselves, not a tourist bar, and sit down with top-quality jamón ibérico, artisanal cheeses and the local accompaniments that go with them. I tell you where the ham comes from, why the acorns matter so much, and how it is produced and carved — in short, why Spaniards consider this the jewel in the crown of their own cuisine. This is where the tour stops being a walk and becomes something you remember.
